**Connect Nexora.** Nexora can now sign in to your [Nexora](https://nexora.app) account. It works like a TV: no password is typed into Nexora. Right-click the island (or the tray icon) → **Nexora** → **Sign in to Nexora…**. The island shows an 8-letter code. Open nexora.app/connect on your phone or this PC, check the PC's name, and choose **Connect**.

Once you're signed in, an **Nexora** page joins the island:

- **Money as it arrives.** When Nexora logs money by itself from your bank's emails or notifications, the island tells you. The sum and sender stay hidden until you click **Show**, so a shared screen shows only "Money in · BCA". To always show them, turn on **Show amounts on the island** in the Nexora menu.
- **Bills before they're due.** Subscriptions and credit-card due dates for the next 7 days, plus a reminder the day before each one.
- **Note a spend.** Type it on the Nexora page ("kopi 25rb") and press Enter. It goes straight to Nexora, and you can Undo it for 10 seconds.

**What this PC can do.** It gets a key of its own, never your password. With it, the PC can see money coming in and bills due, and save the spends you type. It can't read your history, change or delete anything else, or touch your account. The key is stored encrypted for your Windows account. Nexora asks Nexora once a minute while you use the PC, every 3 minutes on battery, and never while the PC is locked or asleep. **Nexora › Disconnect** signs this PC out. You can also disconnect it in nexora.app under Connected apps. A key unused for 90 days stops working.

**Health.** Your steps will come from Nexora on your phone, and that part isn't ready yet. The Health page now says so, whether you're signed in or not.

**Install:** download `Nexora-Setup.exe` and run it. It installs just for you (no admin rights) and can start with Windows. Or unzip the portable zip and run `Nexora\nexora.exe`.

**Upgrading from 0.1.x:** run the new installer. It upgrades in place and keeps your settings and data (`%APPDATA%\Nexora`).

The build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.
