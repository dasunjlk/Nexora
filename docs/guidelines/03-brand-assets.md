## Goal

Replace every upstream brand asset with Nexora's own: logo, app icon,
installer art, phone-page icons and mascot illustrations — all generated by
the ported tools so they can be redrawn at will.

## 03a — logo, icon, installer art (`tools/make_brand.py`)

- The tile becomes a vertical violet gradient (PIL raster + SVG
  `linearGradient`); the island, album cover and equalizer keep their
  geometry; the white Nova mark keeps her geometry (white on violet).
- The installer welcome/finish pictures put the logo tile on a lavender
  field (`FIELD`), replacing the upstream plush photo, which leaves the
  brand kit (and `docs/brand/art/` with it).
- Regenerated: `crates/nexora/res/nexora.ico` (all sizes), the vector logo,
  `nexora-logo-512.png`, Inno wizard/header PNGs, NSIS BMPs.

## 03b — phone page (`server/`)

- `server/make_icons.py` re-runs off `nexora-logo-512.png`; the maskable
  icon's full-bleed ground becomes the tile violet.
- `server/make_phone_art.py` (new) draws the five page illustrations
  (`nova-peek/sit/shelf/asleep/note.webp`) as flat Nexora-palette
  illustrations at the sizes the page's HTML expects, replacing the
  upstream plush photographs.

## Acceptance

- `python -I tools/make_brand.py` and `python -I server/make_icons.py` and
  `python -I server/make_phone_art.py` all run clean.
- No upstream plush photography remains in the repo.
- The icon renders correctly at 16–256 px (spot-checked).
